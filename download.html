Prepcart

Weekly meal plans and shopping lists for your supermarket, your diet and your family.

🤖 Android

Download the Android app (test version)
  1. Tap the button. When the download finishes, open prepcart.apk.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway. Test apps that aren't in the Play Store always show this.

🍎 iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Prepcart from your home screen. It works like an app, also offline.

💻 Computer

Open Prepcart in your browser

Have a free-access code? Open the app, then Profile → Subscription → "Have a free-access code?".

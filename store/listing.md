## Description (both stores, max 4000)
Prepcart plans your week of meals and turns it into a shopping list for your own supermarket, for one person or the whole family.

EVERYONE EATS WHAT SUITS THEM
• Adults and children: children need no age or weight, just their preferences, allergies, portion size and the meals they eat at home.
• Each person has their own diet: everything, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low-carb/keto, Mediterranean or high protein.
• Health and lifestyle needs: low histamine, low FODMAP, MIND (brain health), DASH (heart and blood pressure), blood-sugar friendly, anti-inflammatory, halal and kosher.
• Diets doctors recommend: diabetes, low salt, heart-healthy, kidney-friendly, gout, reflux, low fat, high or low fibre, pregnancy, lactose-free, coeliac and iron-rich.
• Drinks go on the list too: water, juice, coffee, tea, milk and more, per person per day.
• Allergies and foods to avoid are always respected. Dishes that suit everyone are shared; anyone they don't suit gets their own.

ONLY THE MEALS YOU EAT AT HOME
• 1, 2 or 3 meals a day, with snacks chosen separately.
• The same every day, different on weekdays and weekends, or set day by day.
• Lunch at nursery, school or work, a meal out, a fasting day: leave it out, and nothing is cooked or bought for it.

PLAN THE WEEK IN MINUTES
• Over 190 recipes from 16 cuisines, including traditional dishes from your own country, with fresh ideas every week.
• Choose dishes yourself or let Prepcart fill the week, then cook in batches.
• Portions are sized for each person's age, size and activity.

ONE SHOPPING LIST FOR YOUR SUPERMARKET
• Quantities for the whole week, grouped by aisle, with estimated prices.
• Shops in 58 countries; pick real products and prices for your shop.
• Compare several supermarkets: each product goes on the list at the shop where it is the best value.

SHARE IT WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
• Every adult sets their own preferences on their own phone, and it all adds up to one plan and one shopping list.
• End-to-end encrypted: not even Prepcart can read your household's plan.

ORDER ONLINE AND SAVE
• Send your list to a delivery service or open each item in your shop's app, Amazon or Instacart, and finish the order there.
• Weekly saving tips: loyalty apps, own brands, unit prices, frozen and seasonal food, delivery offers.

PREMIUM, ONE PRICE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Budget checker: weekly budget, what you really spent, and cheaper swaps when the week is over budget.
• Compare prices across your shops.
• A private food journal for each adult: meals from the plan, photos, water and your own goals, with an optional PIN.
• A child's food journal for a diet the doctor recommended.

EASY TO READ, FOR EVERYONE
• Light and dark mode, three text sizes and high contrast.
• A dyslexia-friendly mode with the OpenDyslexic font and wider spacing, and a font for low vision.
• Reduced motion, and screens checked against accessibility guidelines.
• 27 languages.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Diets, health details and family members stay encrypted on your phone.
• An account is optional (email, Google, Apple or Facebook); it's needed for household sharing and to use Premium on several devices.
• Food journals and their photos never leave your phone.

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Planning, recipes, every diet, drinks, the shopping list, household sharing and ordering online are free, for any number of people. Try Premium free for a month; after that it's one low price for the whole family (US $6.99 a month), monthly or yearly. Cancel any time in your store account.

Prepcart may earn a commission when you order from a shop through the app, at no extra cost to you.

Prepcart is a planning aid, not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, follow the advice of your doctor or dietitian.

Delete your Weekly Basket account

Weekly Basket by [your name or company]

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Weekly Basket and go to Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes your account, your email address and name, and your subscription record with RevenueCat straight away.

Without the app

Email [support email] from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account (email, name, sign-in method), consent record, affiliate code, and subscription record.
  • Kept without your account id: records of payments linked to an affiliate (amount and date only), for as long as tax law requires (usually 6–10 years).
  • On your device: meal plans, diets and lists never leave your device. Remove them with Profile → "Delete all my data", or by uninstalling the app.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Privacy Policy

Prepcart · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Short version: your meal plans, diets, weights and family details stay on your device, encrypted. If you create an account we keep only your email address, your name, when you accepted our terms and, if you joined through an invite link, the affiliate's code. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is [your name or company], [address]. Contact: [support email]. For anything about your data, write to that address; we answer within 30 days.

2. What stays on your device

To plan meals the app asks for: names (or nicknames) of the people you plan for, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, diet, allergies and foods to avoid, preferred cuisines, country, region and supermarket, chosen recipes, shopping list and prices you type in.

This is stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256 (Web Crypto, AES-GCM) using a key created on the device that cannot be exported. It is never sent to us or anyone else, also when you have an account. Because we never receive it, we cannot see, sell or share it. Health information (weight, height, diet, allergies) is used only on the device to estimate calorie needs and filter recipes.

5. Your rights

  • Access and portability: Profile → "Export my data" downloads everything on your device. For your account data, email us.
  • Correction: change your details in the app, or email us.
  • Deletion: Profile → "Delete my account" deletes your account and its data straight away. Profile → "Delete all my data" erases everything on the device. See also how to delete your account.
  • Objection and restriction: email us.
  • Complaint: you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK the ICO, in the EU your national authority).
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information; you have the right to know, delete and correct, and we won't treat you differently for using these rights.

6. Children

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. A parent or guardian may enter details about children to plan family meals; that information stays on the device.

7. Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores its data and your sign-in on your device (IndexedDB / local storage), which is strictly necessary for it to work.

8. Security

Device data is encrypted at rest. Network traffic uses HTTPS. Account data is protected by access rules so that only you can read your account. The app loads only its own code (plus Google's sign-in helper), enforced by a Content Security Policy.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll show the new version in the app and update the date above.

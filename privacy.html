Privacy Policy

Weekly Basket · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Short version: what you type into Weekly Basket stays on your device, encrypted. We have no accounts, no servers that store your data, no ads and no tracking.

1. What you enter and where it is kept

To plan meals the app asks for: names (or nicknames) of the people you plan for, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, diet, allergies and foods to avoid, preferred cuisines, your country, region and supermarket, your chosen recipes, shopping list and any prices you type in.

All of this is stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256 (Web Crypto, AES-GCM) using a key that is created on your device and cannot be exported. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Because we never receive it, we cannot see, sell or share it.

2. What leaves your device, and why

Sent toWhatWhy
Open Food Facts and Open Prices (non-profit, open data) The search word (e.g. "rice"), your country and your supermarket's name. Your IP address is visible to them as for any website. To show real products and recently reported prices at your supermarket.
Apple App Store / Google Play, and RevenueCat (app versions only) Your purchase and subscription status, and a random, anonymous app user ID. To process and verify your subscription. We never see your payment card details.

No personal profile, health information, family details or shopping list is ever included in these requests.

3. Health information

Weight, height, age, diet and allergies are used only on your device to estimate calorie needs and filter recipes. Calorie targets are estimates and are not medical advice.

4. Children

The app is meant for adults planning for themselves or their family. A parent or guardian may enter details about children to plan family meals; that information also stays on the device.

5. Your control

  • Export: Profile → "Export my data" downloads everything the app holds about you as a file.
  • Delete: Profile → "Delete all my data" erases everything, including the encryption key. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel in your App Store / Google Play account settings.

6. Security

Data is encrypted at rest on the device. The app only loads its own code (a strict Content Security Policy blocks third-party scripts), product images are only loaded from Open Food Facts' image servers, and all network traffic uses HTTPS.
